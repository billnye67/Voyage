What maps reveal

Put every earthquake from the last hundred years on one world map and something jumps out: they’re not scattered. They line up in long, narrow bands — and the volcanoes sit in the same bands. One map, and a planet-sized secret shows itself. Learn to read maps for patterns, not just places.
