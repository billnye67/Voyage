Characters, settings & events

Every story runs on three gears: who (characters), where and when (setting), and what happens (events). The gears aren’t separate — an old rope bridge makes boards crack, and a careful girl reacts to a crack very differently than a reckless one. Learn to read each gear from its details, and watch how they turn each other.
