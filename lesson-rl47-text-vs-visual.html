Text vs pictures

An artist reads the same story you do — then paints what THEY saw. Sometimes the picture matches the words exactly. Sometimes the artist adds a moon nobody wrote, or loses a boat the author counted. Comparing the two versions, detail by detail, is a real reading skill — and it starts with trusting your own eyes.
