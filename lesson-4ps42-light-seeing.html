How seeing works

Deep inside a cave with the flashlight off, you see nothing. Not shadowy shapes — NOTHING. Click the light on, and the whole cavern appears. What actually happens between a rock and your eye that makes seeing possible? Build the model and you’ll know why darkness beats even the sharpest eyes.
