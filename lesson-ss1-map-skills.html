Map skills

Somewhere on Gull Island, a supply cache is buried. You have the island map and one note from the ranger who buried it. That should be enough — if you can read what the map is telling you. Most people can't. By the end of this lesson, you'll read maps like a ranger: direction, symbols, addresses, and distance.
