Science words & their signals

Nonfiction loves big precise words — evaporation, condensation, precipitation. Here’s the secret: good writers almost never leave you stranded. They plant a definition right next to the hard word, marked by little signals — a dash, a colon, the word “or.” Learn the signals, and science words start defining themselves.
