Point of view

Every story is told through somebody’s eyes — and whose eyes changes everything. A race feels different from inside a runner’s pounding chest than from the stands. A prank is hilarious to the prankster and awful to the victim. Learn to spot who’s telling the story, and what their eyes can and can’t see.
