Messages in patterns

Your best friend is camped across the lake — way too far to shout. But you both have flashlights, and last night you agreed on a secret code. Tonight, blinks of light will carry whole messages across the water. Learn how patterns become information, and how to judge which signal wins when the fog rolls in.
