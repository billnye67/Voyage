Area and perimeter

A rectangle keeps two secrets with the same two numbers. Ask “how much fence goes around it?” and you get one answer. Ask “how many squares fit inside it?” and you get a completely different one. Mixing the two up is the most popular mistake in fourth grade — and by the end of this lesson it won’t be yours.
