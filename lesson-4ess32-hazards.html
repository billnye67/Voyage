Designing for disasters

Nobody can stop an earthquake. Nobody can talk a hurricane out of landing. But two towns hit by the very same storm can end up with wildly different damage — and the difference is design. Learn how engineers use maps, evidence, and clever building to shrink what a disaster can do.
