Questions, answered

The short version.

Is the price really for my whole family?

Yes. One membership covers the children in your household, with no per-child fee.

Which subjects are included?

Math, reading and language arts, science, and social studies are all included in both plans.

Does the AI tutor give kids the answers?

It is designed to teach through questions, hints, examples, and alternate explanations first — helping kids do the thinking instead of skipping it.

Can different ages use Voyage?

Yes. Each child follows learning suited to their level and keeps their own progress, projects, and portfolio.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The monthly plan can be canceled anytime. Annual access continues through the end of the paid year.
