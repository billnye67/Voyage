How energy travels

A drum booms across a gym and your chest feels it. The sun sits 150 million kilometers away and still warms your face. A switch on one wall lights a bulb on another. Energy is constantly on the move, and it needs something to travel by. Learn the four carriers behind most of the deliveries around you, and you can trace them hop by hop.
