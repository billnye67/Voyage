Build an energy converter

A rubber band, two wheels, and a milk-carton body: wind it up, let go, and it races across the floor. Nobody pushed it. The secret is a device that CONVERTS energy — from one form into another. Today you design one, test it like an engineer, and use the test data to make it better.
