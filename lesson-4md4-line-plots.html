Line plots

Your class measures 12 pencils and shouts out 12 numbers. It’s chaos — until you stack the numbers on a number line, one X per pencil. Suddenly you can see everything at once: which length is most common, which is rare, how far the longest beats the shortest. One picture, every answer.
