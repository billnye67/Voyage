How we know about the past

Family legend says Grandma walked three miles to school, uphill, in the snow. Everyone repeats it at Thanksgiving. But in a dusty attic box you just found something the legend never counted on: evidence. Time to find out what really happened — and to learn the detective skills historians use on all of history.
