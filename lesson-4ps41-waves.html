Reading waves

Flick a jump rope and a hump races down it. Toss a pebble in a pond and rings spread out in perfect circles. Waves are everywhere — and they all follow the same repeating pattern. Learn the two measurements scientists use to describe ANY wave, and what a passing wave does to a rubber duck.
