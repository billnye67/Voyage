Angles as turns

A skater spins. A doorknob twists. A clock hand sweeps. Every one of those is a turn — and turns have their own ruler. A full spin is 360 degrees, and every angle you’ll ever meet is just a piece of that spin, frozen in place. Once you see angles as turns, their sizes stop being mysterious.
