Explaining what happened & why

Every hour or so, a fountain of boiling water blasts fifty meters into the Yellowstone sky. A good reader can do more than say “a geyser erupted” — they can explain the whole chain: what happened, and why each step forced the next one. Nonfiction is full of these chains. Learn to walk them.
