The 5 U.S. regions

Fifty states is a lot to keep in your head. So Americans use a shortcut: they group the states into five big neighborhoods called regions. Learn the five, and suddenly the whole country fits in your pocket — you'll know what the land looks like, what the weather does, and what people are up to in a state you've never even visited.
