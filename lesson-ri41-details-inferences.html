Facts and inferences

Aquarium workers kept finding their octopus missing at night — and hiding in a teapot by morning. The article never explains HOW it escapes. But if you snap two of its facts together, you can figure it out yourself. That’s reading nonfiction like a scientist: facts you can point to, plus conclusions you can prove.
