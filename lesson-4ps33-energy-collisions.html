Energy in collisions

CLACK. One marble slams into another: the shooter nearly stops, the target rockets away, and the whole room hears it. In that split second, energy changed hands — and none of it vanished. Learn to track every bit of a crash’s energy, and to predict what the next collision will do before it happens.
