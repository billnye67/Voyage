Energy and speed

You can’t see energy. Nobody can. But roll a marble into a tray of sand and energy leaves a confession: a dent. Roll it faster and the dent gets deeper, the thud gets louder. Today you learn to read those clues like a scientist — and to back up a claim about how much energy a moving thing carries, using evidence anyone can check.
