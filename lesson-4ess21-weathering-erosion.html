Breaking down, carrying away

The Grand Canyon is a mile deep, and the only tool that dug it was a river. No explosion, no giant shovel — just water, moving grains of rock away one at a time, for millions of years. Learn the two-step process that reshapes every landscape on Earth, and how to test it in a tray of dirt.
