Line symmetry

Fold a paper valentine down its crease and the two halves land perfectly on top of each other — every edge kissing its twin. That crease has a name. But some fold lines that look perfect secretly fail, and some shapes that look perfectly balanced have no good fold at all. One simple test settles every case.
