Poems, drama & prose

The same rainy afternoon, the same lost dog — written three completely different ways. One version comes in paragraphs, one in short stacked lines, one looks like a script with names in capital letters. Each shape has a name, its own parts, and its own superpower. Learn to tell them apart at a glance.
