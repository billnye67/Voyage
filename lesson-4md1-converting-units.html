Converting units

A big litre jug and a tiny 100-millilitre cup can both fill the same fish tank — you just need different numbers of them. Every measurement works like that: the same amount wears a big number in small units and a small number in big units. Once you know how many smalls fit inside one big, you can switch between them any time.
