Word meanings & myth words

Stories are full of words nobody taught you — and you can crack most of them without a dictionary. The words around them leak clues. And some words are secret doorways to ancient myths: call a chore “Herculean” and a three-thousand-year-old strongman walks into your sentence. This lesson hands you both keys.
