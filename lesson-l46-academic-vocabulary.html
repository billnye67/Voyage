School direction words

Half the battle with any school question is one bossy word at the front: COMPARE this, EXPLAIN that, SUMMARIZE the chapter. Each of those words orders a completely different job — and kids lose points every day for doing the wrong job beautifully. Learn the boss words once; use them in every subject forever.
