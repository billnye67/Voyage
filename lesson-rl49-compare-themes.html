Themes across cultures

A lion in ancient Greece. A crane in old Japan. Different animals, different countries, stories told thousands of miles apart — and somehow they’re arguing the exact same lesson. Themes are travelers: they cross oceans and centuries wearing whatever clothes each culture gives them. Learn to spot one under any costume.
