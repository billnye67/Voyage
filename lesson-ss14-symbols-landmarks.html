U.S. symbols & landmarks

The American flag is a message written in numbers — and most people salute it their whole lives without ever counting. Today you crack the flag’s code, meet the eagle, the statue, and the cracked bell, and learn why a country carves its memory into copper, marble, and mountainsides.
