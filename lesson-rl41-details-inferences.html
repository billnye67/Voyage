Details and inferences

A story tells you some things straight out — and whispers the rest. Rain on the window, a packed beach bag, a long sigh… the text never says “Maya is disappointed,” but you know it anyway. Reading like a detective means knowing which answers are printed on the page, which ones you built from clues — and how to prove both.
