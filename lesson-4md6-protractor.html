Using a protractor

The protractor is the ruler for turns — a half-moon of little degree marks. But it plays a trick on beginners: every mark shows two numbers, like 60 and 120 sharing one spot. Read the wrong one and your answer isn’t just off, it’s backwards. There’s a simple rule that picks the right number every single time.
