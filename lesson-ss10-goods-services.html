Goods, services, producers & consumers

You’ve got birthday money and a whole main street to spend it on. But look closely at what you’re actually buying in each shop — sometimes you walk out holding something, and sometimes you walk out with… nothing in your hands, yet somehow you still paid. Cracking that difference cracks open how the whole economy works.
