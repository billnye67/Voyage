Theme and summarizing

Ask five kids what a story was about and you’ll get one kid retelling every single detail, three mumbling “a bird, I think,” and maybe one who can do the two real jobs: shrink the story down to its bones, and say what lesson it’s secretly arguing. Those two jobs have names — summary and theme — and each has a recipe.
